# Stock Value Lens search queries by page type

Reviewed: **2026-10-21**. Website: [Stock Value Lens](https://stockvaluelens.com/). This is a qualitative query-priority list for English-speaking users researching US stocks. It is not investment advice.

## How to read the rankings

The numbers rank likely query frequency **within each page type**, from higher to lower. They are informed estimates based on common investor tasks, query breadth, and each page's actual content and coverage. No keyword-volume or Search Console export was available, so these are not measured search volumes, traffic forecasts, or organic-difficulty scores. Company-page patterns use `[TICKER]` and `[COMPANY]` as placeholders; use the specific page's issuer and supported metric when applying them. Search intent and demand vary by country and date.

## Overall search-potential order across page types

1. **Company financial-history pages** — company + ticker + EPS, P/E, free cash flow, or historical-financial searches directly match a specific user task; thousands of pages serve long-tail queries.
2. **Homepage and interactive chart tool** — broad free stock research, chart, and history searches have large potential demand, with strong competition.
3. **Stock directory** — ticker lookup and coverage discovery can route company-specific searches to a matching page.
4. **P/E, price-vs-earnings, and export guides** — common historical research tasks with clear informational intent.
5. **Head-to-head comparison tool** — useful for company comparisons, though searchers may more often seek an editorial recommendation or live performance.
6. **EPS/FCF, dividends, and other metric guides** — narrower educational queries that fit the site well.
7. **AI/data resources and catalog** — high fit for programmatic users, but likely a specialized audience.
8. **Methodology and sources** — important for trust and interpretation; generally lower-volume searches.
9. **Overview-only company pages** — useful company-intent matches, but only for business-profile questions, not historical financials.
10. **Updates, about, and privacy pages** — primarily navigational and trust queries with limited non-brand discovery potential.

The overall order is a prioritization hypothesis, not measured keyword volume. Use Search Console query and landing-page impressions after enough data accrues to revise it; use Keyword Planner or another keyword-volume source to validate demand by target country and language.
