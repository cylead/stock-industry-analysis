# Stock Value Lens search keywords and LLM questions

Reviewed: **2026-10-21**. Website: [Stock Value Lens](https://stockvaluelens.com/). Audience assumption: English-speaking users researching US-listed stocks. This is a qualitative priority list, not investment advice.

## Ranking notes

Each page type has two separate lists because they are intended for different tools:

- **Search keywords** are short phrases for keyword research tools such as Keyword Planner.
- **LLM questions** are natural-language prompts for testing discovery and answers in assistants.

Ranks are estimated likelihoods within each list, based on common investor tasks, query breadth, page fit, and current site content. No keyword-volume data or Search Console export was available, so these are hypotheses rather than measured demand, traffic forecasts, or organic-difficulty scores. Company-page lists use `[TICKER]` and `[COMPANY]` placeholders; substitute the specific company and only its supported metrics. Demand varies by country and over time.

## Overall page-type search potential

This cross-site order estimates total discovery potential, separate from the within-page rankings above:

1. **Company financial-history pages** — a large set of ticker-specific pages can match long-tail EPS, P/E, cash-flow, and historical-financial queries.
2. **Homepage and chart tool** — broad stock chart and research terms may have high demand, with strong competition.
3. **Stock directory** — helps users find the right company page and can match ticker lookup searches.
4. **P/E, price-versus-earnings, and CSV guides** — common historical research tasks with clear intent.
5. **Head-to-head comparison tool** — useful for company comparisons, though some users seek live performance or recommendations instead.
6. **Other metric guides** — narrower educational topics such as EPS versus FCF, margins, and dividend yield.
7. **AI/data resources and catalog** — high fit for programmatic use, but a specialized audience.
8. **Methodology and sources** — valuable for trust and interpretation, with lower expected query volume.
9. **Overview-only company pages** — can match business-profile searches but do not provide historical financial charts.
10. **Updates, about, and privacy pages** — mostly brand, navigational, or trust searches.

Validate keyword phrases by country and language with Keyword Planner or a comparable volume source. Review LLM prompts separately in the assistants you care about; search rankings and assistant discovery measure different behavior.

## Pages reviewed

The live review covered the homepage, directory, sample S&P 500 pages with financial charts, an overview-only S&P 500 page, comparison tool, research-guide hub and guides, methodology, sources, AI/data access, updates, about, and privacy. Company-page lists use templates because the site repeats those page structures across tickers. Queries should be limited to the data and coverage shown on the specific destination.
