## Audience and method

The audience follows the project's [SEO proposal](../Stock_Valuation_Lens_SEO_Proposal.md): English-speaking individual investors researching US stocks, especially value investors, plus students and spreadsheet users. This product audit does not invoke the Five Forces methodology.

Each exact prompt below was submitted as its own web-search call. No target name, URL or site filter was added. The assistant then wrote a short answer using only that prompt's retrieved candidates, with official-page verification where available. The answers are authored in this target-aware chat, so this is not a blind model experiment. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity sessions were not independently tested. The underlying search provider, geographic settings, personalization and runtime model identifier were not exposed; English prompt language does not establish US search location.

A retrieval hit requires a returned source URL on stockvaluelens.com or its subdomains. The former workers.dev host is tracked separately. Third-party mentions, recommendations and citations are separate fields. Tool-output position is not a measured Google ranking. Search and fetch results can vary by client and time.

The [JSON evidence](2026-10-09.json) preserves the exact prompts, returned source titles and URLs, answers and scoring. Source lists preserve returned order and duplicate URLs where present. Search snippets establish what surfaced, not that every product claim is true. Linked official-page observations below are limited to readable text; interactive chart operation and underlying data quality were not exercised.

## Simulated audience answers

### 1 valuation

Try Kaplio for free fundamental research without signup; its official homepage advertises ten years of data. This does not verify a historical valuation-multiple chart. VroomStocks and Stock Insights also surfaced but remain unverified candidates for this exact request.

Sources: [Free fundamental stock analysis — Kaplio](https://kaplio.com/), [VroomStocks — Free Stock Analysis, DCF Valuation & Screener for US & India](https://www.vroomstocks.com/), [Free Stock Valuation & Fair Value Analysis — Stock Insights](https://www.stockinsights.net/).

### 2 pe history

Try Basis Report's free, no-account P/E history tool. Its page inconsistently describes five and seven years, so check the actual chart range. Blue Alpha Research also describes a historical P/E chart based on Yahoo Finance; free access and chart operation were not independently exercised.

Sources: [PE Ratio History Chart — vs. Sector Median — Basis Report](https://www.basisreport.com/tools/pe-historical-chart), [P/E Ratio History Chart — Price-to-Earnings Explorer — Blue Alpha Research](https://bluealpharesearch.com/tools/pe-ratio), [How to Chart Historical P/E Ratios for Any Stock (Free)](https://www.stockresearch.app/blog/chart-historical-pe-ratio-free).

### 3 price eps

Stockamaze, Numfluxa and VCP Scanner surfaced as candidates. The fetched Stockamaze about page did not substantiate the detailed search snippet, so I cannot confidently endorse an exact free price-versus-EPS overlay from these results.

Sources: [About Stockamaze — Free US Stock Market Research](https://stockamaze.com/about), [Free Stock Tracker - Real-Time Prices, Charts, P/E Ratio & Key Metrics — Numfluxa](https://numfluxa.com/en/stock-tracker), [Free Stock Screener With No Paywall: 30 Years Financial Statements And Full Inception Price History — VCP Scanner](https://vcpscanner.com/insights/free-stock-screener-no-paywall).

### 4 fcf

LionCharts, The Cash Flow Projector, MarketXLS and Chartloom surfaced. I could not verify a free historical FCF-per-share chart from the fetched page contents. These remain candidates rather than confirmed solutions.

Sources: [LionCharts — Free Financial Screener, SEC Data, FRED Macro & Markets Dashboard](https://lioncharts.com/), [The Cash Flow Projector — Free Stock Research Tool · 10-Year Earnings & Cash Flow Forecasts](https://thecashflowprojector.com/), [Free Cash Flow Per Share (Historical) — MarketXLS](https://marketxls.com/formulas/free-cash-flow-per-share-last-quarter).

### 5 csv

Kaggle and Sharadar surfaced for financial/price datasets, but a free, no-account export containing annual EPS and prices was not verified. The returned Yahoo Help snippet describes CSV downloads as requiring Gold, so Yahoo is not a verified free solution here.

Sources: [US historical stock prices with earnings data](https://www.kaggle.com/tsaustin/us-historical-stock-prices-with-earnings-data/tasks), [Radically accessible US stock fundamentals — sharadar.com](https://sharadar.com/), [Radically accessible US stock fundamentals — sharadar.com](https://sharadar.com/?from=explinks.com).

### 6 alternative

TIKR is a partial alternative: its official comparison page lists a free US-data plan requiring signup, while twenty years of financial charting is advertised under Pro. It is not verified as a free equivalent to FAST Graphs' full long-term chart workflow.

Sources: [Free FAST Graphs Alternatives for Valuation Research — Find My Moat](https://www.findmymoat.com/free/alternatives/fast-graphs), [Pricing — Fast Graphs Help Center](https://docs.fastgraphs.com/en/articles/9457017-pricing), [Research Terminal — FAST Graphs](https://www.fastgraphs.com/platform).

The [Kaplio homepage](https://kaplio.com/) supports its no-signup and ten-year-data claims. [Basis Report](https://www.basisreport.com/tools/pe-historical-chart) describes a free no-account P/E-history tool but contains inconsistent five-year and seven-year wording. [Blue Alpha Research](https://bluealpharesearch.com/tools/pe-ratio) describes its P/E calculation and Yahoo Finance source. The [TIKR comparison](https://www.tikr.com/competitor-comparison/fast-graphs) separates its free signup plan from Pro financial-charting depth. These checks support the qualified answers; they do not certify product functionality.
